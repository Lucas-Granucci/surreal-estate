The themes

✦ Attributed opinions

Walkable, but quiet after dark

PRAISED

One of the most walkable, striking downtowns of any mid-sized U.S. city.

CRITICIZED

Streets empty and restaurants close earlier than in bigger cities.

Groceries are scarce

PRAISED

Park and public-space investment around Market Square and Point State Park.

CRITICIZED

No full-service grocery. Commenters say that won’t change until more people live here. Map data agrees →

Downtown versus Shadyside or Lawrenceville

PRAISED

Convenient, transit-friendly, and cheaper than nearby neighborhoods.

CRITICIZED

Better social scene and community elsewhere, especially for people who drive.

Driving and parking

PRAISED

Heavy bus and rail connections across the core.

CRITICIZED

Owning a car is a hassle. Rush-hour jams at Fifth Ave and Grant St slow surface transit.
