## What to improve before calling every example a flagship

The source acquisition sometimes returns neighborhood marketing or broad dining
directories where substantive resident/customer discussions would be more useful.
The long Penn Avenue corridor creates a particular relevance problem. Sparse
coverage must not become an inflated claim of comprehensive research. Source
dates, review provenance, geographic context, and unresolved parcel matches should
remain visible. A few synthesis-only retries were inspected; they are not assumed
to be better just because they were generated later.

The strongest public story is the whole evidence workflow: map → local brief →
original passages → records → honest coverage. Lead with East Liberty. Keep the
qualified samples as deliberate illustrations of where coverage varies, rather
than claiming five equally strong or independently verified neighborhood studies.
