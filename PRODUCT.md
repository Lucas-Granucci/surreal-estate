# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to become knowledgeable about an address and its locale, both to evaluate a place and investigate its records and context. Confirmed by the user; a narrower buyer or professional role is not assumed.

## Product Purpose

Turn an address into navigable, evidence-backed local knowledge. Help readers find important information quickly, understand what it means, and inspect its sources.

## Operating Context

Local Python web application with saved reports. User-requested workflow: type an address, quickly confirm the match, select research channels, follow generation progress, explore the report.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Public records, mapped nearby places, street-closure permits, and optional AI-assisted web research. Pittsburgh-focused property/permit coverage; other coverage varies. Address geocoding gives an estimated street point. Sources, qualifications, missing data and model-reported content must remain distinguishable. Permit records do not prove work is underway. Saved source JSON can contain useful detail omitted by prior displays.

## Brand Commitments

SurrealEstate. User requests a completely reimagined, minimal, clean, concise interface without a light/dark toggle. Prioritize important information and meaningful visualizations, minimal onboarding text and visible generation progress.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing provider adapters, report schema, saved development snapshots, tests and offline synthetic demo. No invented property findings, travel times, risk ratings or progress percentages.

## Product Principles

- Useful context before processing metadata.
- A small next action at each onboarding step.
- Visualize real data; keep evidence one step away.
- Preserve uncertainty and source distinctions.
- Make depth available without making it the default.
